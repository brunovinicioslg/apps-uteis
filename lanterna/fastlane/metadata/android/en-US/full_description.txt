Alumia turns the flashlight on when you shake your phone. It works with the app closed and the screen locked: take the phone out of your pocket, shake it two or three times, and there is light.

SHAKE TO TURN ON
• Choose the sensitivity (low, medium or high) and how many shakes it takes (2, 3 or 4).
• Works with the app closed, with the screen locked and, if you want, with the screen off.
• A short vibration when the light turns on and a different one when it turns off, so you know without looking.

NO ACCIDENTAL LIGHT
• In a pocket or a bag, shakes are ignored (proximity sensor).
• During calls too.
• Walking, running and slow shakes do not count.

FLASHLIGHT
• Turns off by itself after 1, 5, 10 or 30 minutes.
• Turns off when the battery drops to the level you choose.
• Brightness control on Android 13 or newer, on phones that allow it.

SHORTCUTS
• Quick Settings tile to turn shaking on and off.
• Home screen widget.
• Shortcuts when you touch and hold the app icon.
• Samsung side key (double press) and Pixel Quick Tap.

OPTIONAL NOTIFICATION
To listen for shakes in the background, Android requires the app to keep a notification. If it bothers you, turn off "Persistent notification" in the app: the notification goes away and shaking keeps working.

PRIVACY
• Free, with no ads.
• No internet access: the app does not even have the permission.
• No data collected.
• Open source (GPL-3.0): github.com/brunovinicioslg/apps-uteis

Tip: on Xiaomi and Samsung phones, exempt Alumia from battery saving (the app shows how) so the system does not stop the shake detection.
